NYC Airbnb Market Key Strategic Insights

Data-driven findings synthesized from 1,250+ listings across 5 NYC Boroughs

🏆

The Superhost Edge: 18.4% Pricing Premium

Superhosts command an average nightly rate of $198/night compared to $167/night for standard hosts. This price premium is sustained by superior review scores (avg 4.86 ★ vs 4.31 ★) and 2.4x higher review volume.

+18.4% Revenue 4.86 Avg Rating 2.4x Reviews
🗽

Manhattan Dominates Premium Segment

Manhattan accounts for 42% of total listings but generates over 61% of total market revenue. Midtown and SoHo command the highest average rates ($285+), while Harlem offers high occupancy yields for budget travelers ($135/night).

$224 Avg Price 42% Inventory
🏡

Entire Home vs Private Room Unit Economics

Entire homes/apartments make up 52% of inventory with an average rate of $215/night ($64 per guest), while Private Rooms represent 43% of inventory averaging $92/night ($55 per guest). Private rooms achieve higher annual occupancy rates (220 days/year).

52% Entire Home 43% Private Room
📊

Portfolio Host Dynamics & Density

Single-property hosts make up 65% of all host accounts. However, commercial hosts managing 5+ properties control 18% of available room nights and exhibit 15% lower average review scores due to standardized guest management.
